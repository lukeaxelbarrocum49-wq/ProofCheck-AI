STAY PROTECTED

Cybersecurity Tips

Simple habits that can help you avoid scams and phishing.

🔐

Protect your passwords

Use unique passwords for important accounts and a reputable password manager. Never share one-time codes.

🔗

Check links carefully

Inspect the real domain before signing in. A familiar brand name in a URL does not prove that the site is genuine.

⏳

Do not rush

Messages threatening immediate account closure or promising unexpected prizes may be trying to pressure you.

📱

Verify independently

Contact a bank, delivery service, or organization through its official app or a number you already trust.

🛡️

Enable two-factor authentication

Add an extra layer of protection to your accounts whenever it is available.

🔄

Keep devices updated

Install operating-system, browser, and application security updates regularly.

💳

Never pay to claim a prize

Be cautious of unexpected fees, gift-card payments, cryptocurrency transfers, or requests for banking details.

📢

Report suspicious activity

Report suspected scams to the relevant platform or financial institution. If you entered a password, change it through the official site.
